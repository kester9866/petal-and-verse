A NOTE ON BOTANY & FLORIOGRAPHY

Rooted in science.
Written in feeling.

植物分类与花的寓意，分别阅读。

Orders and families follow APG IV; names and garden examples are checked against Kew and RHS references. Botanical classifications can change as research develops.

目、科参考 APG IV；属名与代表种参考 Kew、RHS。分类会随研究更新，条目内说明部分新旧名称关系。

Flower meanings belong to cultural traditions. We distinguish a historical lexicon from contemporary florist usage, and leave unsupported meanings open.

花语是文化约定，不是植物学属性。历史花语与现代花店用语标明出处；没有可靠依据的条目以植物资料为主。

Species are representative examples, not exhaustive lists. French texts are translations of the cited traditions. The ink drawings are decorative, rather than identification keys.

代表种不是完整种名录；法语是引用内容的译文，并不表示当地普遍采用这一花语。线描用于审美，不能代替植物鉴定。
